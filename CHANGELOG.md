# Changelog

All notable changes to Allure Analytics. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## v0.1.0 — first public version

See where your site stands on speed against your competitors, and which quick wins move you up.

### Home
- Your position in one positive sentence ("You're 3rd. Next step: 2nd place, 8 points away."), the PageSpeed curve beside the standings, each score with its change since your last analysis.
- Quick wins as a live what-if: today, the score within reach, the gain and the place; tick or untick actions and every number follows, all combinations adding up exactly.
- Moments: the rank rolls when it changes, the standings slide into place, a place won glints; an analysis that ends on screen lands with your rank very large, then into the sentence.
- After a first analysis, the standings take the whole card and a quiet line marks the starting point.

### Compare
- Score breakdown: where each site's points go, across the five Lighthouse measures.
- Real visitors first: 6-month Chrome UX Report curves, standings on the real value, share of good visits against Google's 75% mark.
- The lab page by page, each value opening Google's own PageSpeed report.

### Measurement
- PageSpeed Insights v5 and Chrome UX Report, straight from the browser with your free Google key; like-for-like comparison; ties under 2 points; the PageSpeed score recomputed with Lighthouse's scoring, to the rounding.
- A four-step guide to the Google key, checked as soon as it's pasted (one light request per API, nothing measured).
- "Measured by Google ⓘ": how the score is made, and why Lighthouse in your own Chrome scores higher.

### Product
- Opens on the real product; the demo is one click away (and `#/demo` links straight to it), told apart by a dark band.
- French and English (`?lang=en`), light and dark, phone to desktop, zero dependencies, no build step.
- Tests on mocked Google APIs, run on every push.
